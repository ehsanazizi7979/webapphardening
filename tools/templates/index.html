How the two books differ

Alice & Bob Learn Secure CodingWeb Application Security (Hoffman)
Best forDevelopers who want a practical checklist, from first job to seniorDevelopers and testers who want to understand attacks deeply
ApproachAdvice-first: “here is what to do and why”, organised by topic and by SDLC phaseAttacker-first: recon, exploit, then measure-by-measure defence
Structure3 parts: general advice → language/framework specifics → secure SDLC3 parts: recon → offense → defense, plus a history chapter
CodeSnippets in PHP, JavaScript, Python, SQL, Java, C#, Ruby, and moreMostly JavaScript, chosen to keep examples readable
StrengthBreadth of practical guidance; exercises turn it into a courseDepth on how each vulnerability actually works; strong threat modelling and vulnerability-management chapters
Read it whenYou are writing code today and want rules you can apply immediatelyYou want to think like the person attacking your code
